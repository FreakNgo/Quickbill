Privacy Policy

QuickBill by Antify Studio · Last updated 13 November 2026

The short version
  • Your shop details, products and bills are saved only on your own phone or computer.
  • QuickBill has no sign-up and no account. We do not run a server that receives your data, so we cannot see it, sell it or share it.
  • No ads, no tracking and no analytics.
  • Because we do not have a copy, we cannot get your data back for you if you lose it.

1. Who we are

QuickBill is a simple billing app for small shops. It is made by Rajveer Nanadikar at Antify Studio. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Antify Studio, and “you” means the person using the app.

2. What QuickBill saves on your device

To work, the app keeps the following in your browser’s local storage. This is a small storage area inside your browser, on your device. The app does not send it anywhere.

  • Shop details: your shop name, your UPI ID, and your address or tagline.
  • Products: product names, prices and whether you pinned them.
  • Bills: the items, quantities, prices, discount and total, the bill number, the date and time, whether it was marked Cash or UPI, and the shop details at the time of the bill.
  • Your current unfinished bill, so it is still there if you refresh the page.
  • Your preferences: language, light or dark mode, paper width for printing, and whether you have seen the welcome tutorial.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your customers’ names, phone numbers or any other personal details. A bill only contains items and amounts.
  • We do not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.
  • We do not collect bank account numbers, card numbers or passwords.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and QuickBill does not set cookies.

4. UPI payments and QR codes

When you generate a bill, QuickBill builds a UPI payment QR code on your device from your UPI ID, your shop name and the bill total. Anyone who scans or photographs that QR code can see those details, so only show it to the customer you are billing.

The customer pays inside their own UPI app and bank. QuickBill never handles money and cannot see, confirm or track a payment. The Cash or UPI tag on a bill is only your own note. Always check your bank or UPI app to confirm that you have been paid.

5. Printing and Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used only when you tap “Print via Bluetooth”. Your browser asks you to choose a printer, and the receipt is sent directly from your device to that printer. We do not see it and we do not store it. Printed receipts can show your shop name, address, items, totals and UPI ID.

6. Other companies involved

QuickBill does not send your shop, product or bill data to any of these companies. They can only see the technical details that any website visit reveals, such as your IP address and browser type.

  • Google Fonts. When you are online, the app asks Google’s servers for the fonts it uses. Google handles that request under its own privacy policy. If the fonts cannot load, the app uses your device’s standard fonts and still works.
  • Our website host, Vercel. When you open QuickBill or this page, Vercel delivers the files and may keep normal server logs under its own privacy policy.

The QR code maker is built into the app, so creating a QR code does not contact anyone.

7. How long your data is kept

We do not keep any of your data. Everything stays on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • delete a single bill with the bin icon in History, or remove every bill with “Delete all”;
  • delete or edit products in Settings;
  • remove everything at once by clearing the site data for QuickBill in your browser settings, or by uninstalling the app from your home screen.
Deleted data cannot be restored. Clearing your browser data, switching phones or losing your phone also removes your bills and products, and we cannot recover them because we never had them.

8. Keeping your data safe

  • Your data stays on your device and is not sent over the internet by the app.
  • The app does not lock or encrypt its data. Anyone who can open your phone or browser may be able to see it, so use a screen lock and be careful on shared devices.
  • Double-check your UPI ID in Settings, because customers pay to that ID.
  • QuickBill is a simple billing helper, not accounting or tax software. Keep your own sales and tax records as the law requires for your business.

9. Your choices

You are in control of everything the app stores. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to look up, correct or delete. If you have a question or request, contact us using the details below.

10. Children

QuickBill is a business tool and is not meant for children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how QuickBill handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects you in a big way, we will say so inside the app too.
