# QuickBill Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

QuickBill is made by Rajveer Nanadikar | Antify Studio.


## In short

QuickBill does not collect, send or sell your data. Everything you
enter stays on your device.


## What is stored

Your shop name, UPI ID, address, products and saved bills (items,
amounts, time, payment mode and the shop details at that time).
This is kept in your browser's local storage on your own device. We
cannot see it.

QuickBill does not ask for customer names, phone numbers or any other
personal details of your customers.


## What we do not do

- No accounts or sign-up
- No analytics or tracking
- No ads
- No cookies set by QuickBill
- No bill or shop data sent to us or to any server of ours


## Third-party requests

When you are online, the app loads fonts from Google Fonts and the QR
code library from cdnjs (Cloudflare). Like any website, these
providers can see your IP address and device details. QuickBill sends
them no bill or shop data. The service where you host the app may also
keep normal server logs.


## Payments

QuickBill creates a UPI QR code from your UPI ID and the bill amount.
The customer pays in their own UPI app. QuickBill does not process,
see or confirm payments. The Cash or UPI tag is only your own record.


## Bluetooth and printing

Bluetooth is used only when you tap Print via Bluetooth and choose a
printer. The receipt goes straight to that printer. Printing through
the print dialog is handled by your device.


## Your control

You can delete bills and products inside the app. To remove
everything, clear the site data for the app in your browser settings.
There is no backup, so deleted or cleared data cannot be recovered.


## Children

QuickBill is a business tool and is not directed at children.


## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
